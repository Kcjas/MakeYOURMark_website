## Scope
Single route `/` (app/page.tsx). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: SUTD community arriving via poster QR on a phone. Job: know when/where/what in seconds, then show up. Constraints: SGT only, no data collection, no map, no invented claims, Instagram URL pending.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is a student zine sheet pasted together on the craft table mid-session; every fact is a physical scrap attached to the page. Refuses the centred hero + three equal icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Sketchbook paper #F3F1EC with grain, ink #1B1A17; pipe-cleaner tomato #EF4E34, sunflower #FFC21F, cobalt #1E4FD8, grass #2F9E47; masking tape, cork #C9945C board. Torn/pinked paper masks, flat offset drop-shadows following the cut edge, rough.js ink lines, Londrina Solid display, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body.
STORY: Visitor sees the name, date, time, place and a live countdown at once; learns it's free and skill-free; picks a table; clears doubts in the FAQ; leaves knowing to walk into Campus Centre.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Make/Your/Mark on three overlapping rotated torn strips filling the width, a pipe-cleaner wire threading them, a taped tag with Mon 16 Nov · 5:30–8:30pm · Campus Centre, rubber-stamp countdown below. No CTA button: the action is showing up.
FORM: Student zine cut-and-paste on a crafting table (position 3 of my grounded list), seed:f40d2a25a234f819. Raises: every label is physically attached by tape, pin, staple or clip (quilt seam discipline); one fixed set of rotations and offsets used everywhere (lattice discipline); the name fills the width at collision scale (fight-poster discipline). Signature interaction: countdown digits stamp in like a rubber stamp; notes wiggle on hover.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
