# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) + Tailwind CSS v4, deployed on Vercel. Libraries where they fit: Motion (animation), Radix Accordion (FAQ), Rough.js (hand-drawn strokes), clsx.

## Users

SUTD students and staff who scan a QR code on a campus poster, almost always on a phone, usually walking past or deciding what to do that evening. Their job: find out when, where, what it is, and whether it's worth dropping by.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for MakeYourMark, a free walk-in craft session. It tells people the date, time and place, what they can make, and answers the obvious questions so they just show up. Success is someone reading it in under 30 seconds and walking into Campus Centre.

## Positioning

A chill, no-skill-needed student craft night run by students, not a workshop or a class. Everything you make, you take home.

## Operating Context

- Event: MakeYourMark, Monday 16 November 2026, 5:30pm–8:30pm SGT.
- Venue: Campus Centre, SUTD. Audience already knows where it is; no map or directions needed.
- All times are SGT only; no time-zone conversion.
- Three stations: pipe cleaner flowers, cotton-stuffed squishies, a shared hand painting board.
- Light snacks provided. Open to the SUTD community only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free, walk in, no sign-up. No registration, no personal data collection, no prizes, no tracking embeds.
- Live countdown to the start; switches to a "happening now" state during the event and a thank-you after.
- Mobile-first; must be fast and pass accessible contrast.
- Instagram link: not yet provided (placeholder in `lib/content.ts`).

## Brand Commitments

- Organised by EnROOT Group 6 as part of ROOT (SUTD student government). Footer credits ROOT and "site by Thejas".
- Voice: a student talking to other students. Short, specific, a bit playful. No marketing buzzwords (unleash, elevate, seamless, immersive, journey), no rhetorical-question headlines, no "Whether you're X or Y".
- Visual brief (user-pinned): handmade crafting table mid-session; saturated pipe-cleaner colours on off-white paper with near-black ink; no gradients, no purple/indigo/violet, no cream+serif+sage; hand-drawn SVG doodles only, no emoji icons, stock icon sets or AI images; minimal motion (hover wiggle, countdown stamp).

## Evidence on Hand

No photos, logos, testimonials or attendance numbers. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Date, time, place first; everything else is a bonus.
2. Say it like a friend would, in as few words as possible.
3. Feels like the event itself: handmade, a bit messy, never corporate.
4. Collect nothing from visitors.
